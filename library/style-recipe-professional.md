# Professional

For a site whose job is to be trusted: services, education, health, money. The page stays quiet and orderly so that the evidence (who is behind it, what qualifies them, what it costs, how to reach them) is what the eye lands on.

## What makes it

- Two to four facts a visitor could check (years, results, numbers, outside ratings) set larger than the body text, near the top. Qualifications before adjectives.
- The real people, named and photographed, with what qualifies them; reviews carry a name, a role and a date, and an outside rating is linked.
- Nothing hidden: prices or a range, terms and what happens next are on the page before any form; more than one way to get in touch, from every screen, with a reply time the business will keep.
- Polish matches the size of the business. One practitioner writes as "I" and shows their own face: no "our team", no chat widget.
