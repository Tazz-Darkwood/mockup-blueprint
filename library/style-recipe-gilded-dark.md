# Gilded dark

For a guild, a raid team, a dice shop or a joke about game classes: anything that should feel like a fantasy game's screen. The page is the game's interface laid over a bright painted world, with a party of heroes out in it. Heroic, chunky and a little silly.

## What makes it

- The first screen is a game screen, not a poster: a player frame (a round portrait and bars), an action bar of chunky square buttons that is the menu (icon, label and key number), a quest tracker listing the page's sections, and loot panels for what is on offer, all over the painted world. Never a framed title beside one figure holding a light.
- The rarity ladder: where things are better or worse, rarer or commoner, their names take the genre's colours in order (grey, white, green, blue, purple, orange), with the word written too ("Epic").
- Bars that run out: anything that fills and empties is a thick bar in its game colour (green health, blue mana, yellow energy, red rage), with the number written on it.
- The genre, never one game: a quest-giver's voice and the genre's free words (mana, loot, raid, class names); two or three sight gags in the painting. Never any real game's name, maker, world, characters, art, icons or font files; everything is drawn for the site.

## Notes from the owner

- On the brief: try "replicating the feels and looks without breaching the IP or trademark", using "the general terms from the game since they are pretty free use".
- On the first try, where the top was a framed title beside one hooded figure: it "still really feels like the same as the [dark painterly guide's] one cuz of the banner and the drawn person with the orb".

## Not covered yet

- Ordinary buttons deep red with gold lettering beside the bright gold main action; the colour pick gives the gold only.
- Big shapes, chunky everything: frames, bars and icons a little too big and too thick. The picks give firm controls and cut corners, not the overall size.
- A party of several chunky heroes doing something in the painted world: the pictures pick gives silhouettes, not the party.
- See-through panels over a busy painting are where contrast fails; keep them dark enough over the worst of what shows through.
- What is fixed and what may change for a second look: the frames, panels, rarity order and bar colours are fixed; the metal, heading and button colours may change (steel and ice in place of gold and red is `{"start": "gilded-dark", "neutrals": "night", "accent": "cobalt"}` in the colour part).
- Phones (the action bar along the bottom, the player frame along the top, the quest tracker a short list that opens): judgement, untried. Inner pages, long reading, shops.
- Only one game of the genre was studied. The sorting of genre from property is judgement, not legal advice.
