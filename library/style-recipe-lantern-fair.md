# Lantern fair

For a street-food night, a market, a fair or a small shop of handmade goods that wants to feel warm-lit and crowded. The light decides how bright the page is: one lantern, and the rest falls into a deep, coloured dark. Bold comes from saturation and cut paper, not from brightness.

## What makes it

- One paper lantern is the page's only light. Its amber is kept for the lantern and the main buttons; nothing else glows, and there is no string of bulbs, because that is many lights.
- The name on two cut blocks turned a degree or two, in dull canvas colours rather than bright ones, and every section heading on its own block. The lettering pick suits playful and dramatic sites; it is here on purpose, because poster letters on dull blocks under one lamp read as friendly at night.
- The stalls as paper tags hung on string from an oak rail under a striped canvas, each with its own drawn dish; when and where as numbered bays on tarmac with painted lines.
- The busy decoration is kept dim: chalk-pale signs at low strength in the margins, bunting in dull band colours strung along every wave, never lit and never amber.
