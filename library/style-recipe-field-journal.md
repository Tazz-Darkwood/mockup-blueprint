# Field journal

For a bird club, a walking group, a garden survey or a nature project run by its members. The page is the notebook: paper and ink everywhere, with the colour and the sky kept for one taped-in sketch. Outdoorsy, unhurried, welcoming.

## What makes it

- One sunny sketch taped in; everything else is paper and ink. The sky, the sun and the saturated colour live only inside that sketch, with the one thing that matters (a heron) ringed in pencil. The page carries the daylight as a faint warm wash from the top right and every shadow falling down and to the left.
- Pencil marks that count things: tallies for places left and birds seen, rings round the notable ones, ticks for "might see", ringed step numbers, a FULL stamp. Marks are robin rust and never pressable; kingfisher blue is only for actions.
- The ground is the notebook's own page: a faint blue-green grid over grained buff paper, showing in every gap.
- The next walk's notice carries a pencilled route map; each walk is a torn, taped sheet with a kraft date tag.
