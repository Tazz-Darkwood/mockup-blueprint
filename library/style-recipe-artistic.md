# Artistic

For a site that should feel like a gallery: a museum, a studio, a poet, an artist. The seven studied sites look nothing alike; what they share is where the detail goes. This recipe keeps the page neutral and quiet so the signature and the work carry it.

## What makes it

- The first screen is one strong idea that fills most of it, taken from the subject: the best picture of the work, or, on a site of words, a piece drawn, lettered or composed for it. Name it in one sentence in the brief before drawing.
- The repeated item (a work, a poem, an exhibition) is styled as an object in the site's own material. Design one until it could stand alone as a picture, then repeat it. Never a plain white card.
- Colour comes from the work being shown, or from one bold flat block. No decorative palette that has nothing to do with the subject.

## Notes from the owner

- On a poetry site: "The fancy top part was really good, so the header and top part are important for artistic sites."
- On the same site: "I really like the ink style but other than the top it's rather plain", and "I really like the boxes for the print studio one also, that worked really well."

## Used on

A poetry site (an ink look, a brush circle at the top, poems framed as small prints); a soap maker's shop (pen frames, torn edges).

## Not covered yet

- Materials: the repeated item's material comes from each site's subject, so no material is picked here.
- Artistic is not hand-made. A one-person craft site also needs the maker's hand and voice (tape, pen lines, the first person), which this recipe does not give.
- The motion pick is from research only: no mockup has tried it.
- Navigation text must not be copied small: four of the seven studied sites set it at 12px or less.
- Inner pages and the top of other pages (a lighter echo of the signature) were not studied.
- Not yet tried as a second guide under another lead.
