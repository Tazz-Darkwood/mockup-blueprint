# Repair cafe

For a repair café, a tool library, a makers' club or any volunteer group that meets in a hall and works with its hands. It is the warm look turned up to full: every surface worked, with warm things, on a bright page. Neighbourly, handy, cheerful.

## What makes it

- The ground is the hall's pegboard shadow board: holes, the shape of each tool drawn round in marker where it hangs, and small things to find in the gaps (a darned sock, a jar of buttons, the hall teapot). It stays bright yellow: the detailed ground takes this look's colour, never a dark one.
- Everything a visitor could bring is a brown repair tag tied on string, like the tag on each broken thing at the door, each in its own colour with its own drawing; the next date is one big tag beside the slogan.
- Fixers are names on a sign-up sheet with empty dotted lines still to fill, the year's repairs are tally marks, and a new sign-up writes its name onto the sheet.
- A slogan in the group's own voice on a red cut-paper banner, beside a drawing of the fixers at their table standing in for a real photograph; between sections, a cloth tape measure and bunting cut from mended scraps.
