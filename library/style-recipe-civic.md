# Civic

For a council, a public body, a charity's forms or any service where everyone must be able to use the page, on any phone, at any reading level. It has no feel guide behind it: it is the parts' civic starting points put together.

## What makes it

- One thing per page. Each page answers one question or asks for one thing, and the next step is one green button that says what it does.
- Help and errors sit next to the field they are about, in plain words, and an error is also summed up at the top of the page.
- Nothing is there to charm. The page is remembered for working: the service's name in the header, then the task. Its one signature is its clarity, so no part is turned up for show.
