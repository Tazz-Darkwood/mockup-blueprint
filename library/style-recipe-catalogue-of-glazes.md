# Catalogue of glazes

For a potter, a weaver, a jeweller or any maker whose work should be looked at slowly. The page is a museum catalogue: a toned paper, short labels, space between pieces, and nothing raised. Quiet, unhurried, tactile.

## What makes it

- Each piece drawn flat and large in its own glaze, dipped to a wavy line over bare clay (the way this maker finishes a pot), one to a screen, set left, centre and right in turn. Real photographs replace them later, shot on the same toned paper in flat, even light with no props.
- A hairline height marker beside each piece ("30 cm"), as in a catalogue of ceramics, with number, name, glaze, size and whether it is available in the margin column.
- The maker's seal (the stamp pressed into each foot) as the site's mark, beside the name and in the footer, and the maker's own voice in the first person.
