Light: the options

Every option in the light part guide, drawn live. Words always sit on a calm patch that passes the contrast minimum; the light is behind or round them, never under the letters.

Flat and even

No light at all

One flat ground, one soft shadow used the same way everywhere. Every pair of text and ground is measured once.

Daylight

Everything lit

A bright sky and one sun, the brightest point. The words go on a dark panel, never on the sky.

Moonlight

Cool and quiet

The same scene at night: a pale moon, everything dim and blue. Measure text near the horizon, where the sky is lightest.

Sky by the clock

The sky keeps time

The big sky behind this box is set from your own clock. Contrast must pass at the worst hour.

One light

A lamp in a dark room

Near the lamp is lit in its colour; the rest falls into dark. The main button takes the lamp's colour.

Spotlight on the main thing

One thing lit

Point at the button, or tab to it: the light grows.

Glow on edges

Bright rims, small things

The glow does not count towards contrast: the letters' own colour must pass. Rare, epic, legendary
