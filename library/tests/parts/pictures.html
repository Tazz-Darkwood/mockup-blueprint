Picture style: the layers

Six layers, each switched on its own: how pictures are made, what is done to them after, how icons are drawn, how heavy every drawn line is, how people are shown, and what fills a photograph's place in a mockup. Every option is shown on a light page (left) and the same CSS on a dark page (right), with the other layers at their defaults; the starting points at the end are saved sets of picks. The drawings were made by the script that builds this page, from the shared colour names only.

Technique

How the pictures are made. The same jug, cup, sprig and sun in each, so only the technique changes. Drawings made from the band colours stay the same picture on a dark page; drawings made in the page's own ink and paper turn over, like chalk on a board.

Flat shapes

technique: flat-shapes

Clean flat shapes in a few colours, no outlines and no shading.

Cut paper

technique: cut-paper

Shapes cut from coloured paper and laid on each other; uneven edges, a little shadow.

Ink line

technique: ink-line

A pen outline with loose flat colour under it, slightly off the line.

Pencil

technique: pencil

A graphite sketch with a light wash: drawn on the spot. One thing ringed.

Ink wash

technique: ink-wash

Brush and ink: dry strokes, soft grey washes, a lot left empty.

Painted

technique: painted

Opaque paint in big shapes: light from one side, shade on the other, brush marks.

Engraved

technique: engraved

Black lines only: tone made by ruled hatching, as on an old printed plate.

Photograph

technique: photograph

A real photograph of the real thing. Here: free to use, credited below.

Treatment

What is done to a picture after it is made: the photograph and one drawing, each with the same treatment. All of it is CSS over the picture, in the shared colours.

None

treatment: none

The picture as it is.

Duotone

treatment: duotone

Two colours from the page: the darks in a band colour, the lights in the pale on it.

Grain

treatment: grain

A fine speckle over the picture, so it sits in a made page.

Halftone

treatment: halftone

Printed in dots: big dots in the shadows, none in the lights.

Riso

treatment: riso

Two inks printed one over the other, slightly out of register.

Aged

treatment: aged

Faded and warmed, softer at the edges, with grain: an old print.

Figures

How people are shown in the pictures. The same scene: people at a table with a jug.

None

figures: none

No people in the pictures: things and places only.

Silhouettes

figures: silhouettes

People as dark shapes against the light: no faces.

Simple

figures: simple

Round heads, dot eyes, a few shapes: friendly, and anyone.

Detailed

figures: detailed

Faces, hair, folds and shade: particular people.

Photographed

figures: photographed

Real people, photographed, named, and asked first.

Stand-in

What fills a photograph's place in a mockup until the real one exists.

None

stand-in: none

Nothing stands in: the drawing is the real picture of the site.

Brief box

stand-in: brief-box

A box the size of the photograph, saying what it must show.

Captioned

stand-in: captioned

A drawing in the site's own style, with a caption that says a photograph goes here.

Quiet

stand-in: quiet

A drawing with an ordinary caption; the alt text and the blueprint say it stands in.
