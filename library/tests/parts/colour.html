Colour: the layers

Five layers: tone, neutrals, accent, strength and bands. Every option is drawn at all five tones (light, toned, mid, dark, lamplit) with the other layers at their defaults, because tone takes the place of the light and dark pages of the other books. Each tone and each starting point is shown beside the look a site would switch to (its other look: the same picks with the tone changed). The test at the foot works out the contrast of every combination of the layers.
