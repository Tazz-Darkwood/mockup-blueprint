Motion: the layers

Every option of every layer, then every starting point, each on a light page and on a dark one. Motion cannot be seen in a picture, so each swatch plays from its Play button, and under it the same motion is drawn stopped at a few moments. Things that move by themselves are already moving. If your device asks for less motion, everything here is an instant change.

Pace

The shared durations and easing curves every other layer uses. The faint dots are where each runner is every 50 ms on its way across. The curves show how far a thing has gone (up) against time (across): arriving (solid), leaving (dashed). The bars are the three durations to scale.
