Frames and edges: the layers

Six layers, each switched on its own: the border round a sheet, the edge of the sheet itself, the edge between two sections, ornament on the border, the dividers between items, and the edge of a field. Every option is shown on a light page (left) and the same CSS on a dark page (right), with the other layers at their defaults; the starting points at the end are saved sets of picks. In each: a sheet with a short form, a list of three items, and the next section below on a band of colour. Shadows are a stand-in for the light part, and corners are square unless a corners part sets them.
