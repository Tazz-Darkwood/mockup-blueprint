Light: the layers

Five layers, each switched on its own: where the light comes from, the shadows raised things cast, what glows, the colour of the sky's light, and where the eye is led. Every option is shown on a light page (left) and the same CSS on a dark page (right), with the other layers at their defaults; the starting points at the end are saved sets of picks. In each: a raised sheet with a button, a stack of three cards (the top one torn and taped), and a ball that shows the bright side.
