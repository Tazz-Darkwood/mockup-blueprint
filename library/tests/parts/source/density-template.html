Density: the layers

Five layers, each switched on its own: the size of the gaps, how full each screen is, how things are arranged, how wide the page runs, and the rhythm from one section to the next. Every option is shown on a light page (left) and the same CSS on a dark page (right), with the other layers at their defaults; the starting points at the end are saved sets of picks. The spacing options are shown in words at real size. The others are shown as a page in miniature: the same page laid out at full size in a window 1600 pixels wide and on a phone 390 wide, then scaled down, with words drawn as bars and pictures as tinted blocks. A dashed line marks the bottom of each screen.
