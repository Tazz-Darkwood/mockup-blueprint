Background: the layers

Five layers, each switched on its own: texture, pattern, drawings, things to find and scene. Every option is shown on a light page (left) and the same CSS on a dark page (right); the starting points at the end are saved sets of picks. The words always sit on a calm sheet.

Texture

None

texture: none

One flat colour; nothing between the reader and the words.

Grain

texture: grain

A fine speckle, like paper or plaster. From across the room, still one colour.

Felt weave

texture: felt-weave

A faint dot weave: the page is a noticeboard's felt.

Mottled and hatched

texture: mottled-and-hatched

Slow patches, fine hatching and grain: an old, worked surface.

Pattern

None

pattern: none

No large shapes behind the sections.

Coloured bands

pattern: coloured-bands

Each section on a whole band of a deep colour, edge to edge.

Pale words can sit straight on a deep band.

Stone courses

pattern: stone-courses

Courses of dressed stone, with a crack or two.

Drawings

None

drawings: none

A few

drawings: a-few

Three large signs, set by hand, one running off the edge.

Lots

drawings: lots

Small signs all over, and larger ones set by hand: something in every corner.

Things to find

Off

finds: off

Nothing hidden in the gaps.

On

finds: on

Small things drawn for the site, sitting in the gaps beside the sheet.
