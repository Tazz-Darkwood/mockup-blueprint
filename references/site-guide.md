# The site's own guide

Every mockup made or restyled with the style stack has a guide of its own beside it, named `<name>.style.md` and recorded in the blueprint as `project.style.site_guide`. It is the top layer of the stack: it holds what is true of this site and nothing else, and it wins over the guides below it everywhere except the accessibility minimums.

It is written for the person who will build the site and for whoever changes its look later. Keep it to what was decided and why. These are its sections, in order; leave one out only when it truly does not apply.

```markdown
# <Site name>: site style guide

**Stack:** the guides this site stacks, lead first, and which parts of the site each one governs.

## Brief
The eight lines the general guide asks for, and who agreed them and when.
If nobody has agreed them yet, say so: the brief is then a proposal, with a question in the blueprint asking whether it is right.
Then the answers to the questions the feel guide asks before drawing (for the warm guide: what would this organisation pin to a wall, and what is it made of).

## Where the detail goes
A table: each part of the page, its level (signature, styled or quiet), and what it is on this site.
One signature piece. Name it in a sentence.

## Not a template because
How many of the general guide's template tells are on the page, and three things on it that were made for this site alone.

## Tokens
Type (which faces and why these, the sizes), colour (each colour and its job), space, finish.
Why these, in a sentence or two: the look comes from the subject.

## The first screen on a phone
What it holds, in order, at 390 by 844, and why.

## Wider screens
Where the layout changes, and what changes.

## Not settled
Stand-ins (a made-up name, placeholder artwork), anything the guides did not decide, and what the owner has not seen yet.

## What the owner said, and where it went
A table: date, the comment in their words, what was changed, and which layer of the stack the point was recorded in.
```

Two rules for keeping it true:

- When an answer or a critique changes the look, change the guide in the same sitting as the page.
- A point of criticism goes to the lowest layer where it is still true. Most belong here. Only what would hold for every site of this kind goes into a stacked guide, and only what would hold for any site at all goes into the general guide.
